# Development asset credits

The 20 sample photos in `data/development_photos/` come from the Role A Rules 1.0.0 handoff. They are public examples for development and demonstration only, not an independent formal test set. Ten are from [TACO](https://github.com/pedropro/TACO) and ten are from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/).

For the individual credit for any photo, find its `image_path` or `case_id` in [`data/development_cases.csv`](data/development_cases.csv). The corresponding row records its author (`image_author`), title (`image_title`), original source (`image_source_url`), licence (`image_license_url`), and any crop or conversion (`image_notes`). TACO records also provide the original image and annotation IDs. Role D copied these files unchanged from the Role A handoff. Each image remains under its recorded licence, including ShareAlike terms where applicable. Preserve the per-photo credits and licence information when reusing or redistributing the photos.

[`data/sources.md`](data/sources.md) records the rule sources and further photo provenance.
